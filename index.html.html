Sarah P. Sutton

Live solo performer

Originally from the Philippines, she is now proud to call San Antonio home. Music has always been an important part of her life, and singing remains one of her greatest passions. Over the years, she has had the pleasure of performing at various community, cultural, and special events.

Outside of music, she enjoys spending time with her husband and son, exploring San Antonio, and discovering new places throughout the city. She continues to pursue her passion for music and welcomes opportunities to share her voice through performances that celebrate community, culture, and connection.
